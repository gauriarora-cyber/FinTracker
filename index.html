🌱 Start Early

Money basics for kids, students and first-time learners. Learn the ideas first — there's no buying or selling here.

🛡️ Before you start

  • Learn with a parent or guardian. Kids shouldn't open accounts or invest on their own.
  • Only ever think about money you won't need soon. Pocket money for school stays for school.
  • Nobody can tell what a price will do tomorrow — not FinTracker, not an AI, not a friend online.
  • If someone promises "guaranteed double money", it's a scam. Tell an adult.
