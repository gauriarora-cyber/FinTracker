Money rules for young investors

  1. Learn with a parent or guardian. Kids don't open accounts or invest alone.
  2. Only think about money you won't need soon. School money stays for school.
  3. Nobody knows tomorrow's price — not FinTracker, not an AI, not a friend online.
  4. "Guaranteed double money" = a scam. Tell an adult!
